## VICIdial Setup

<Note>
  VICIdial requires configuration specific to your instance. Contact support
  from the [Objectionly integrations page](https://app.objectionly.com/dashboard/integrations)
  and our team will confirm the required access, coordinate the setup details,
  and start the connection process for you.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the VICIdial integration">
    In Objectionly, go to **Dashboard** → **Integrations** and select
    **VICIdial**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contact support">
    Click **Contact support** in the VICIdial setup dialog. Include your
    workspace name and the best point of contact for the VICIdial instance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Coordinate setup">
    Support will review the requirements for your VICIdial instance, confirm
    any credentials or endpoint details needed, and guide the implementation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
